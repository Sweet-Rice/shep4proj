CHEM 1101 Principles of Chemistry I

This is an Integrative Learning Core (ILC) course that awards general education credit. Credit or registration in MATH 1021 , MATH 1022 , MATH 1023 , MATH 1431 , MATH 1510 , MATH 1530 , MATH 1550 , or MATH 1551 .  The first part of a three-semester sequence of General Chemistry. The study of matters and measurement, atoms and molecules, chemical bonding, and introduction to chemical reactions.
